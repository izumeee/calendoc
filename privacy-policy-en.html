Calendoc Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Calendoc ("the app") is a calendar app for iOS / macOS developed by izumeee, an independent developer ("the developer"). The app is built on one design principle: your schedule content is never sent to the developer's servers.

1. Calendar and Reminder Data

  • The app reads and writes the calendars and reminders on your device via Apple's EventKit.
  • The app processes this data on your device and never sends it to the developer's servers (the developer operates no servers).
  • Syncing with iCloud, Google, or other services is performed by your device's operating system or the services you have connected, under their respective privacy policies.

2. Google Account Integration (Optional)

If you enable the Google Calendar enhancements (working location, event colors, RSVP, Google Tasks), the app requests your permission through Google's OAuth flow.

  • The app obtains your Google account identifier, email address, and display name to identify and display the connected account.
  • Account information and auth tokens are stored securely in your device's Keychain only and are never sent to the developer's servers.
  • The app communicates directly from your device to Google APIs. No developer-operated or other intermediary server is involved.
  • Information obtained (events, tasks, working locations, etc.) is used solely to display and edit your data — never for advertising, profiling, or sharing with third parties.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the account information and auth tokens from your device. You can also revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Calendoc's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Anonymous Usage Statistics (Opt-out Available)

To improve the app, Calendoc may collect anonymous usage statistics via TelemetryDeck (e.g., which screens are opened, which features are used).

  • The content of your events — titles, locations, attendees — is never transmitted.
  • Collected signals are anonymized and cannot identify you. The app performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • You can turn this off anytime in Settings ("Send anonymous usage statistics").

4. Data the Developer Does Not Collect or Use

  • The developer does not collect events, tasks, working locations, or Google account information on developer-operated servers.
  • The app does not access your device contacts or current location.
  • No advertising SDKs.
  • Events, tasks, working locations, and Google account information are never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated, and material changes will be announced in the app.
